# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove material or restrict participation to maintain a welcoming environment.

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Use [GitHub’s reporting tools](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) to report conduct concerns. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.
